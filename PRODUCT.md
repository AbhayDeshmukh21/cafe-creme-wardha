# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Medical and college students from Datta Meghe Medical College & Hospital and neighboring Wardha institutions looking for a relaxing hangout between classes, study sessions, and hospital shifts.
- Local residents, families, and young professionals in Sawangi and Wardha seeking quality coffee, specialty cold beverages, and indulgent desserts.
- Visiting friends and groups looking for a comfortable, welcoming meeting spot with signature treats.

## Product Purpose
- Provide a vibrant, inviting digital presence for the physical Café Crème outlet in Sawangi, Wardha.
- Showcase Café Crème's signature creations (Kuka Royale, Dalgona Crème, Sizzling Brownie, Red Fizz Lime Soda) and café atmosphere.
- Drive in-person café visits by providing immediate, friction-free access to operational details: exact location near Datta Meghe Medical College, opening hours (10:00 AM – 10:30 PM daily), Google Maps navigation, and Instagram updates.
- Success means visitors feel welcomed, easily discover the signature menu highlights, and navigate effortlessly to the café in person.

## Positioning
The premier youth, student, and coffee-lover hangout in Sawangi, Wardha—distinguished by Café Crème's genuine signature recipes (especially the thick, chocolatey Kuka Royale and whipped Dalgona crème) and a warm, hospitable vibe situated conveniently right near Datta Meghe Medical College.

## Operating Context
- Physical brick-and-mortar café in Sawangi, Wardha, Maharashtra, operating daily 10:00 AM – 10:30 PM.
- Primary digital access happens on smartphones: students and visitors browsing on mobile while on campus, in transit, or deciding where to meet.
- Complemented by an active social channel (@cafe_creme_wardha on Instagram) for community engagement and visual updates.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single-page responsive web experience with fluid Lenis smooth scrolling, GSAP entrance and scroll reveals, and an interactive 4-product 3D-feel podium showcase.
- Section architecture: Hero, Our Story, Signature Collection, and Visit Us / About Us, supported by fixed/sticky navigation and comprehensive footer.
- Focus is strictly on brand presentation, menu discovery, and visitor logistics; no online food delivery cart, checkout, or ordering backend is currently required.
- Confirmed terminology: "Café Crème", "Lip Lickin' Happiness", "Kuka Royale", "Dalgona Crème", "Sizzling Brownie", "Red Fizz Lime Soda", "Sawangi, Wardha".

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Café Crème Wardha
- Tagline: "Lip Lickin' Happiness"
- Eyebrow: "LIP LICKIN' HAPPINESS — EST. WARDHA"
- Tone of Voice: Warm, genuine, enthusiastic, inviting, and community-focused.
- Visual Assets: Branded takeaway cups, rich chocolate/beverage product renders, warm neutral podium aesthetic.

## Product Principles
1. Destination-First Utility: Everything on the site supports a seamless transition from screen to seat—location, directions, hours, and ambiance are always clear and accessible.
2. Spotlight the Signatures: Let Café Crème's iconic creations—especially the thick crunch of Kuka Royale—lead the visual narrative.
3. Warm, Student-Friendly Welcome: Embrace the energy of the college town; the experience is inviting, unpretentious, and celebrates "little moments of happiness".
4. Mobile-First Fluidity: Ensure instantaneous load times, responsive touch-friendly podium interactions, and clean navigation on mobile devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` settings across all Lenis and GSAP animations so visitors sensitive to motion experience clean, stable presentation.
- Maintain high text contrast ratios against light beige and cream backgrounds for optimal legibility.
- Semantic HTML landmarks, clear ARIA labeling for interactive product podium callouts, and descriptive alt text for all photography.
